# Code of conduct

This community follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome newcomers, criticize ideas and code — never people, and assume good intent.
Harassment, insults, discriminatory language and sharing other people's private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **conduct@docodez.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, issues or pull requests, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break
these rules.
